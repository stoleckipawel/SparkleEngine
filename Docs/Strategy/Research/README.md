## Public Engineering Study

Read these in order when selecting a real-world precedent:

1. [Engineer Profiles](GraphicsEngineerProfiles.md) — twelve public engineering profiles, affiliation confidence, personal/team attribution and an excluded identity. Former employment and historical biographies remain explicit.
2. [Role Levels](GraphicsRoleDepth.md) — eight employer role families; senior ownership, expert depth and principal scope as a documented synthesis, with an evidence-review rubric.
3. [Rendering Examples](RenderingReferenceExamples.md) — twenty what/where/why cards, including sixteen commit-pinned source repositories, license locations, local consumers, falsifiers and transfer limits.

The [delivery catalog's exercises](../FeatureDeliveryCatalog.md#research-informed-delivery-exercises) turn these sources into concrete remaining-work outputs. Study sources to understand assumptions; do not copy their frameworks or treat public GitHub activity as hiring proof.
